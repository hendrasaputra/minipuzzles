# Picture Islands

Fill squares to reveal a picture. Each clue shows a group of filled squares. A clue of **3 1** means three filled squares, at least one blank, then one filled square.

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Fill a square | Choose **Fill** and tap a square |
| Mark a blank | Choose **Mark empty** and tap a square |
| Play with a keyboard | Open **Flat board**, then use the arrow keys |
| Move the camera | Choose **Rotate** and drag |
| See examples | Open **Menu → How to play** |

Choose one of eight levels or start a random Easy, Medium, or Hard puzzle. The random boards are 5 × 5, 6 × 6, and 8 × 8. Each puzzle has one solution. Hint marks one square. Undo reverses the last edit. Retry keeps the layout. Progress is saved in this browser.

**See example** beside the board shows a completed clue of **2 1**. The first picture highlights the middle column, then the middle row. Both have a clue of **5**, so fill every square in each line.
