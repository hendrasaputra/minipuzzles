## Prompt

Create one original premium puzzle-game app icon as a standalone 1024×1024 PNG. Use a tactile, Apple-inspired skeuomorphic rounded-square ivory ceramic base containing a miniature isometric puzzle diorama. The style is an original interpretation of carefully crafted desktop app icons, never a copy of an existing app icon or an Apple logo. Use an orthographic 30-degree elevation and 45-degree yaw, a coherent scale, softly beveled chunky geometry, subtle ceramic/wood/brass material texture, gentle ambient occlusion, and one warm soft key light from the upper left. Shadows fall consistently toward the lower right. Favor warm ivory, blush pink, muted sage, dusty blue, and warm brass; use the specified game accent sparingly. Keep the miniature centered with comfortable padding, while the ivory backing fills the canvas. Use an opaque square ivory ceramic backing filling the canvas; the website applies a26% rounded-corner mask. Do not add an outside backdrop or dark matte. Keep the subject inside that mask. Limit the miniature to a few large readable objects, with a clear silhouette at 64×64. No title, letters, logos, badges, border frame, UI screenshot, gradients used as decoration, harsh neon, excessive gloss, or clutter. Produce exactly one icon, not a contact sheet or phone mockup.

Use the crafted skeuomorphic quality of classic Iconfactory app icons as the design reference: a distinctive miniature physical object, precise bevels, rich but restrained materials, warm believable light and a readable silhouette at 64px. Keep the original puzzle subject and the shared isometric camera. No Iconfactory/Apple logos or copied existing icon compositions. See [the shared style reference](../ICON_STYLE.md).

Game subject: A miniature floating 3×3 board of chunky blush-pink voxel tiles, two cream revealed tiles with simple embossed pips, and one tiny coral flag on a brass pole. One raised back corner suggests a second connected floor. No numerals or lettering.

Accent family: #e7adc5. Preserve the shared ivory base, camera, lighting, scale and visual weight from the style contract. Make the puzzle mechanism readable through objects, not written explanation.
