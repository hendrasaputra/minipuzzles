# Pocket Railroads

Send each train to the station with its letter. **Train’s station** tells you the destination. **Your route ends at** shows where the current switches will send it.

1. Change switches until the two station letters match.
2. Send the train and watch it follow the rails.
3. Set the route for the next train.

A train sent to the wrong station returns to the yard. Trains that reached their stations stay delivered. Hint sets one switch or helps you send a train. Undo reverses the last switch change or delivery.

Open Menu to choose from eight levels or start a seeded Easy, Medium, or Hard puzzle. Hard puzzles have more junctions and trains. Retry keeps the same layout and train order. Reload restores your progress.

Use Rotate to look around the 3D board. The train travels along the rails with small steam effects. Reduced motion shows the result immediately.
