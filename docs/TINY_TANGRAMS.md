# Tiny Tangrams

Fit every piece inside the outline. Cover it without gaps or overlaps.

1. Choose a piece. The preview shows where it would land.
2. Use **Turn** to rotate it a quarter turn.
3. Tap the board to place it. You can also choose a column and row and press **Place**.

In **First square**, place the first triangle at **A1**. Turn the second triangle until it fits at **A1**, then place it. Open **See example** for the visual steps.

Use **Lift piece** to remove a placed piece or **Undo** to reverse the last edit. Hint helps place a piece. Rotate turns the 3D view. Menu offers eight levels and seeded Easy, Medium, and Hard puzzles. Progress is saved in this browser.
