# Marble Workshop

Turn the tracks. Tap the marble or press **Roll**. Get the marble into the cup.

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Turn a track | Tap it, or choose it and press **Turn** |
| Test the route | Tap the marble or press **Roll** |
| Fix a gap | Turn the track beside it and roll again |
| Turn the board | **Rotate**, then drag |
| Play another puzzle | **Menu → Levels** or **New random puzzle** |

Open **How to play** in Menu for four animated examples. On the keyboard, Enter or Space on the board turns the selected track in Play mode.

There are eight levels. Random puzzles take 2–4 turns on Easy, 5–7 on Medium, and 8–11 on Hard. Hints turn one track. Retry keeps the layout. Reload restores the puzzle, turns, and completed levels.

The marble rolls through straight tracks and curves. Dust follows it and sparks appear at the cup. Reduced motion shows the result immediately.

Marble Workshop is available at `/games/marble-workshop/`.
