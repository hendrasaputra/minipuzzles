# Flip Garden

Make **Your garden** match **Goal**.

Tap a tile to flip it and the tiles directly above, below, left, and right. A bare tile grows a flower. A flower becomes a bare tile. Diagonal tiles stay as they are.

In the first level, flip **B2** to grow a cross of five flowers. Use the row and column labels to find the same tile on the goal map and board. **See example** shows the five tiles that change.

Choose **Rotate** and drag to look around. Choose **Play** to flip tiles. Menu has eight levels and seeded Easy, Medium, and Hard gardens. Hint marks a useful tile to flip. Undo reverses the last flip. Retry keeps the same pattern. Progress is saved in this browser.
